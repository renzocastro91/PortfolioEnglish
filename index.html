About me



I'm a Backend Developer with 2 years and 4 months of experience at Rosario Software, where I design and build RESTful APIs and web applications for clients in the logistics, banking and retail sectors, such as Correo Argentino, Banco de Formosa and Claymore-Lowlands.

I work mainly with Python and TypeScript with Nest.js, and I'm trained in Java, Spring Boot and MuleSoft. I have also built data and AI solutions: machine learning, OpenAI API integration, customer segmentation and sales forecasting.

I'm part of an Agile team that works with Scrum, and every feature I build ships with its unit tests (Jest and Pytest). I also use AI-assisted development tools (Claude, Codex and Cursor) in both work and personal projects. I'm studying Information Systems Engineering at UTN.
